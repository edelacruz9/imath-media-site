FAQ

Good questions.

Where do you shoot?

Jacksonville and surrounding Duval, St. Johns, and Clay county areas. Outside that range, ask us for a quick quote.

What services do you offer?

Every package includes a vertical 9:16 reel and a horizontal MLS tour cut. Higher tiers add drone aerials, edited photos, twilight footage, an agent on camera reel, and labeled virtual staging. Floor plans and 3D tours are not offered at this time.

How do I book a shoot?

Choose your home size and package, pick a date and time in the booking form below, and send your request. We confirm your time shortly after. You can also call 703.963.2829 or email admin@imathinc.com.

What is your turnaround time?

Our target is 72 hours from your shoot. Add the rush option if you need it sooner.

Can you accommodate rush or same day shoots?

Rush delivery is available as an add on. Same day shoots depend on our schedule, so call 703.963.2829 and we will do our best.

What do I receive?

A vertical 9:16 reel for social media and a horizontal tour cut for the MLS, both with licensed music. Higher packages add drone footage, edited photos, and more.

Do you include photo editing?

Yes. Signature and Premier packages include 25 to 30 edited photos, color corrected for a bright, clean, natural look. The Essential package is video only.

Do I need to be at the property?

Not always. Many agents arrange access with a lockbox code. Add access details in the notes when you book. A clean, well lit home makes the biggest difference, so use the shoot prep checklist above.

What if the weather is bad?

Exterior and drone footage depend on weather and wind. If conditions are not good enough, we will help you reschedule.

Do you use AI?

We use technology to speed up editing, captions, and formatting. The footage of your home is real. Any virtually staged image is clearly labeled, and we can provide the original.

Do you offer virtual staging?

Yes. Premier includes up to 5 virtually staged images, and extra images are $25 each. Staged images are always labeled.

Can I use the video on the MLS and social media?

Yes. Your delivered video and photos are for marketing your listing on the MLS, your website, and social media.

Can I reschedule?

Yes. Let us know as early as you can and we will find a new time.

How do I contact you?

Call 703.963.2829 or email admin@imathinc.com.
